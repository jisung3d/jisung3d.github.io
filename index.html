3D COMPUTER VISION

Jisung Yoo

Senior Research Scientist @ NAVER LABS

3D Reconstruction · Neural Rendering · Generative 3D

I am a Senior Research Scientist at NAVER LABS, working on large-scale 3D reconstruction and novel-view synthesis for real-world environments. I have contributed to NAVER Map Street View 3D and FlyingView 3D, as well as reconstruction for city-scale digital twins and media/VFX applications. My current research explores how generative models and feed-forward 3D representations can complement geometric reconstruction and neural rendering.

Before joining NAVER LABS in 2022, I worked at Samsung Electronics and Samsung Research on mobile AR, computational photography, and 3D content creation. At Samsung Research, I led an incubation team exploring AR/VR experiences. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from KAIST in 2018, with doctoral research on interactive 3D reconstruction using RGB-D cameras.

CURRENT RESEARCH

Generative Priors for 3D Reconstruction

Ongoing research

I am exploring how priors from existing generative video and 3D models can refine incomplete 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstructions. The focus is on poorly observed regions and views beyond the capture trajectory, such as pedestrian-level and aerial views of scenes reconstructed from street-view imagery. The aim is to improve geometry and appearance while keeping the results consistent across views.
