CURRENT RESEARCH

Generative 3D &
Novel View Synthesis

Ongoing research

Exploring generative video/3D diffusion models and feed-forward 3D Gaussian representations as complementary priors for geometry-based reconstruction, particularly for under-observed regions and viewpoints beyond captured observations, with an emphasis on geometric and multi-view consistency.
